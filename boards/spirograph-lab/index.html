Spirograph Lab: two numbers, one drawing

A gear rolls inside a ring and a pen rides on the gear. Choose the gear's size as a fraction of the ring and how far out the pen sits. One choice draws a perfectly straight line. Can you find it?

Experiments

  1. Find the straight line. Try gear 1/2. Move the pen to 1, right on the rim. What does the pen do?
  2. Count the lobes. Try 1/3, 1/4, 1/5. How does the number of points relate to the gear's bottom number?
  3. Count the laps. Try 3/8, then 5/8. How many times does the gear go round the ring before the drawing closes?
  4. Pull the pen in. Keep the gear and slide the pen from 1 to 0. When do the sharp points turn into loops, and then into smooth waves?
  5. Make your own. When you find one you love, copy your numbers and post them under the video. We draw the best ones in a Short.

The maths: a gear of radius r rolls inside a ring of radius 1. With r = p/q in lowest terms the drawing has q lobes and closes after p laps. At r = 1/2 with the pen on the rim, every point of the gear's edge moves on a straight line: the Tusi couple, described by Nasir al-Din al-Tusi in the 13th century.
