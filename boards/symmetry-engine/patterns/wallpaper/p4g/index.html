The p4g wallpaper pattern

Quarter-turns and mirrors in two directions, but the quarter-turn centres are not on the mirrors.

How to spot it

Pinwheels of four, arranged so that mirror lines run between them.

Where you see it

Bathroom linoleum; painted Chinese porcelain; fly screens.

Conway's code: 4*2

turn 4 $3/4 + * $1 + corner 2 $1/4 = $2
