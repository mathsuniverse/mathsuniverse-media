# Format: "5 Most ___" countdown where the maths fights back

Taken from Mrwhosetheboss, "5 Most Painful Alarm Clocks 😭" (tNSjkqtwwnM, 59 s, 107.7 M views, 5 Jun 2025), analysed 1 Nov 2026. Razvan: "These 3 ideas are the kind of topic we need to pick up."

## The spine
1. Title = the whole promise: "5 Most [emotion word] [everyday thing]" + emoji. Countdown = progress bar, nobody leaves before #1.
2. Frame 0 shows ALL FIVE items at once, with a big "5".
3. Each item, about 10 s, three beats: the rule ("to make it work you must…"), ONE hard number, a live attempt.
4. The attempt FAILS on screen (gap appears, counter of tries climbs). The maths fights back; the failure is the entertainment.
5. Strict escalation, mild to extreme; #1 is the only one with the big claim/danger number.
6. Everyday stakes (your floor, your dice, a puzzle on a napkin).
7. Dense voice (~2.7 words/s), a visual change every 1–3 s, minimal on-screen text.
8. No outro: cut on the #1 reaction and loop to frame 0.

## First three
- 5 Shapes That Refuse to Tile Your Floor 😭: circles (90.7% max coverage), regular pentagons (108°, 36° gap), octagons (need squares), Penrose kite+dart (1974, never repeats), the hat (2023, David Smith et al.: one tile, never repeats). Links: Symmetry Engine wallpaper/Penrose labs.
- 5 Most Unfair Dice 🎲: ending on non-transitive dice (A beats B beats C beats A).
- 5 Puzzles You Can't Finish 😤: Königsberg bridges, three utilities, 15-puzzle swap, … ; #1 proven impossible.
