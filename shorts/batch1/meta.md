## 3. B2, Tusi: "Circle illusion"
Title: Circle illusion: 32 dots, all moving in straight lines
Description:
One dot slides along a line. Add a second line, then 4, 8, 16, 32: every dot still moves only in a straight line, yet together they roll as a circle. It is a gear half the size of its ring: the Tusi couple, drawn by Nasir al-Din al-Tusi in 1247.
