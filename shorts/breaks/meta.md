The young universe made matter and antimatter in pairs. For roughly every billion particles of antimatter there was about one extra particle of matter (CERN), and everything you see is made of that leftover. The imbalance was first caught in 1964 in kaons (Cronin and Fitch); why the universe tipped is still open.

## B3 · Life picks a hand (break6.mp4)
Title: Why is life left-handed? Chirality, Pasteur and DNA #shorts
Hook: Your two hands / can never match
Description: lab + full video lines, then:
Mirror-image molecules are like your hands: you can't turn one into the other. In 1848 Louis Pasteur sorted left- and right-handed crystals by hand. Life's amino acids are left-handed, the sugars in DNA right-handed, and the double helix twists to the right. How life first chose is still an open question.
#chirality #chemistry #dna #biology #symmetry
Tags: chirality, homochirality, why is life left handed, pasteur, enantiomers, amino acids, dna right handed, symmetry breaking

## B4 · From a ball to a body (break7.mp4)
Title: How does an embryo know where its head goes? Turing patterns #shorts
Hook: How does a ball of cells / grow a head?
Description: lab + full video lines, then:
In 1952 Alan Turing showed that an even mix of chemicals that react and spread can break its own symmetry into spots and stripes. Processes like that, with cues already in the egg, lay out head and tail, back and belly. Animals with one mirror line appear about 555 million years ago.
#turing #biology #embryo #symmetry #maths
Tags: turing patterns, reaction diffusion, morphogenesis, alan turing, embryo development, bilateral symmetry, symmetry breaking

## B5 · The heart moves left (break8.mp4)
Title: Why is your heart on the left? #shorts
Hook: Why is your heart / on the LEFT?
Description: lab + full video lines, then:
In the early embryo, spinning cilia in a tiny pit called the node push fluid to the left (Nonaka et al., Cell, 1998). That flow tells the body its left from its right. Fully mirrored organs (situs inversus) happen in about 1 in 10,000 people.
#heart #biology #anatomy #embryo #symmetry
Tags: why is the heart on the left, situs inversus, cilia, nodal flow, left right asymmetry, embryo, symmetry breaking
